I'm firstmost a dilettante, flaneûr, and dandy. Deeply unserious in the most serious way. I have this life and I'm determined to live in beautifully. I have recreational interests in mathematics, physics, philosophy, and dance. I've created this website as a way to collect my studies, and also share myself with the world. This is one more breadcrumb by which I hope likeminded people will find me. In the last year I've begun tweeting; it's a good way to get to know me.

I want to learn to be a good person; towards that end, if you have constructive advice you're encouraged to write it in my Admonymous.
